Sprint 903 Phase 5 -- Flyweight content-node cache benchmark

Standalone, self-contained page: no server, no app needed. Compares element.innerHTML = htmlString (today's approach in NoteTableController.js's buildCellsFromSelector()) against parsing each distinct content string into a detached master Node once and using node.cloneNode(true) for every subsequent cell that needs the same content. See 903-phase-5-flyweight-content-cache-plan.md for the full design writeup.

The 24 content variants below were extracted directly from a real captured console log (903-notes-for-flyweight-cache.log) -- not synthetic data. In that real capture, 300 total per-cell rebuilds resolved to only 24 distinct content strings (each repeated 12-13 times), which is why the defaults below are 300 cells / 24 variants.
