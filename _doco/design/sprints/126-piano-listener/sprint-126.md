## Purpose

Purpose of this sprint is to: Make Piano Lineage Instruments able to be Listeners via wiring to multi-stringed Instruments.  Stragegy is to ignore row and column, and just use midiNum / MIDI pitch, with low-to-high and high-to-low algorithms.
